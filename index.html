Machine Learning · Healthcare

Cardiovascular Risk Prediction

02
Objective
Model heart disease risk (classification) and LDL cholesterol levels (regression) from 15,000 patients and 19 clinical variables.
Approach
Feature engineering, cross-validation and model comparison: penalized regressions (LASSO, Ridge, Elastic Net), SVM, boosting, neural networks, Random Forest and CART.
Result
AUC between 0.76 and 0.81; penalized linear models offered the best balance of performance and interpretability, with a pipeline optimized to minimize false negatives.
